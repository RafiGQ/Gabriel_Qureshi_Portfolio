About me

UX designGraphic designAccessible designDesign systems

I am a third-year Web and User Experience Design student at Manchester Metropolitan University, interested in where UX meets graphic design and psychology.

I base my design decisions on user research so I am not relying on my own assumptions. I design in Figma and build in HTML and CSS. I use Adobe software for brand and graphic work.
